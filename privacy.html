Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Adage Dance Media is a dance photography and videography business serving Orange County and the Inland Empire, California. This page explains what information we collect when you contact us, and what we do with it.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us:

  • Website contact form: your name, how to reach you (email or phone), the type of shoot, your date, and any details you add.
  • Facebook and Instagram lead forms: your name, email address, phone number, and your answers to the questions on the form, such as your studio name, show date and approximate number of dancers.
  • Email, phone and messages: whatever you include when you write, call or text us.

Our website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure and running.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To schedule and deliver the work you book.
  • To follow up with you about that inquiry or booking.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a marketing list without asking first.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run the business, and only as needed for them to do that:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers website contact form submissions to our inbox.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), which hosts our lead forms and holds the responses you submit there.
  • Google Workspace, which provides our email.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and booking details for as long as needed to respond to you, complete the work and keep ordinary business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. If you are a California resident, you have rights under California privacy law, including the right to know what we have collected and to request deletion. To make a request, email us and we will respond promptly.

Children

Our contact and lead forms are intended for adults, such as studio owners, teachers, parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect contact information from children under 13 through these forms.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
